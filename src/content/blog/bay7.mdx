**Available Space:** Approximately 100,000 sq. ft. The bay runs 1,269 ft. 8 in. long, with 80 ft. 6 in. of clear width inside the columns and an 84 ft. 10 in. column-center crane span.

**Former Use:** Steel mill / Quality Bar operations, including cold bar and turned-ground-and-polished bar handling.

**General Layout:** Long, clear industrial bay with overhead cranes, high-bay lighting, a heavy-duty concrete floor, and road access along the south side.

**Truck Access and Doors:**

* East end overhead door: 20 ft. 3 in. wide by 25 ft. 8 in. tall.
* West end overhead door: 14 ft. 7 in. wide by 15 ft. 5 in. tall.
* South-side overhead door: approximately 48 ft. wide.
* Man doors are located near the overhead doors.

**Cranes and Material Handling:**

* One 35-ton single-hook overhead crane at the west end.
* Two 15-ton twin-hook overhead cranes at the center and east end.
* Approximately 22 ft. of clearance to the bottom of the crane hook.

**Utilities:**

* FirstEnergy / Ohio Edison electric service: 800 amp service, 23,000 to 24,000 volt primary, 480 volt secondary, with switchgear along the north columns.
* A 1,000 KVA three-phase General Electric transformer with 23,000 volt primary and 480Y/277 secondary.
* East Ohio / Enbridge natural gas: a 4 in. gas line with medium-pressure service up to 20 PSI.
* Campbell city water serves the facility.
* On-site compressor equipment, including a Gardner Denver 75 HP compressor.

**Rail and Site Access:** Rail runs into Bay 7 from the north side of the property; the siding needs tie replacement before active use. A road runs along the south side of Bay 7, and a newer truck-capable bridge over the Mahoning River serves the site.

**Condition Notes:** The floor is thick, heavy-duty concrete built for heavy loads. Raised slab sections, troughs, and pits remain from previous equipment, and the west end needs additional work. The roof was recently repaired and coated. There is no permanent office or bathroom inside Bay 7.
